AI Wingschool 02 · The AI Era

The AI era

AI didn't arrive now. It's been arriving for seventy years.

What changed is the speed. The last three years moved AI from research labs into your child's homework, your inbox, and every job they'll apply for.

The evolution

1956
Rule-based

Computers told exactly what to do, if-this-then-that. No learning, no judgment.

1990s
Machine Learning

Machines start learning patterns from data instead of being hand-coded.

2012
Deep Learning

Neural networks unlock vision, speech, and translation at human levels.

2022
Generative AI

ChatGPT arrives. Machines write, design, and reason in language.

2024+
Agentic AI

AI doesn't just answer, it acts. Plans, decides, ships work across tools.

2026
Embodied AI

AI moves into the physical world, humanoid robots, smart homes, machines that see and act around your child.

What's here now

Three kinds of AI your child will use this year.

Creates
Generative AI

Writes essays, designs images, generates code from a sentence. The kind your child meets in ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney.

Acts
Agentic AI

Doesn't just answer, it does. Plans a trip, books the flights, drafts the code, ships the result. The shift defining 2025 onward.

Senses
Multimodal AI

Sees, hears, speaks. Reads a maths problem from a photo and explains it aloud in Hindi. Tutors that finally feel human.

AI Wingschool 08 · Questions parents ask

Questions parents ask

Honest answers, not sales pitches.

Will this interfere with school or board exams?

No. We designed the cadence around Indian school life, about 30 minutes of self-paced work a day, plus a 2-hour weekend workshop. Most students fit it around tuitions. During exam weeks, the self-paced layer pauses without penalty.

Does my child need to know coding?

Not at all. We start from zero. The first month is about thinking with AI, experimentation, prompting, scoping problems. Technical confidence grows through making, not intimidation.

Do we need to buy any hardware ourselves?

No. Every project that needs hardware (microcontrollers, sensors, motors, robotics modules) ships free to your doorstep across India. If something breaks during the build, we replace it at no charge. After the program ends, the entire kit stays with your child.

Can I enrol multiple children?

Yes, and the second child gets 10% off, the third or more get 20% off. Discounts apply to AI Academy and AI Architect, are confirmed at signup, and don't require both children to be in the same season. The Trial Pass is already low-friction at ₹999, so we don't stack sibling discounts on top of it.

How is this different from YouTube or self-learning?

Three things: a live mentor who calls them out, a real cohort that creates accountability, and a portfolio they have to ship. Free content can inform, it almost never produces follow-through. We've measured this across 11 cohorts.

AI Wingschool 08 · Questions parents ask

Questions parents ask (continued)

More from the demo call, asked verbatim.

Who actually teaches my child?

Mentors are graduates of IIT Madras, IIT Ropar, IIM Bangalore, and IIM Ranchi, most with industry experience at AI-first startups. Cohorts are capped at 25 so every student gets seen and challenged personally.

What if my child has already used ChatGPT?

That's a great starting point, but not the finish line. Wingschool moves them from casually using AI to directing it, critiquing it, and building real products with it. Most students who arrive 'already familiar' learn the most from the engineering layer.

How will I, as a parent, see progress?

Three ways. (1) Project deliverables every 3 weeks, actual working artefacts, not grades. (2) A parent progress report every 2 weeks on the AI Academy and AI Architect plans. (3) Flagship Expo Day at the end, you'll watch your child present what they built.

What happens if it doesn't work for us?

Full refund within 7 days, no questions asked. After that, we let you switch plans, pause, or cancel mid-cohort if life changes, and if you paid upfront, we refund pro-rata for the months you didn't use. We'd rather have a happy alumni family than an unhappy paying one.

Is this only for students who want to work in tech?

No, it's for any student who wants agency in the next decade. We've had students go on to bio, design, journalism, and policy. The skill we teach (using AI to build) compounds in every field.
