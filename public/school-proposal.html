WingsQuest 2026 · How it works

One initiative. Four phases. Zero cost to your school.

From a one-hour challenge in your school hall to your students on a public stage, here is the whole arc.

01

The Challenge.

WingsQuest 2026: a free AI aptitude test, hosted at your school, run entirely by our team. Free for the school. Free for every student. Every participant receives a certificate; your school receives a full performance report.

02

The Selection.

Students who clear the cutoff qualify for the AI Builder Program. The top 10–20% earn scholarships and school-assembly recognition. Every qualifier is eligible to enrol.

03

The AI Builder Program.

A 4-week live online program. Students learn how modern AI works and build a real project of their own, taught by IIT/IIM graduates.

04

Students present their projects to a panel of industry experts and IIT/IIM alumni. Finishers receive project-completion certificates, presented at Expo Day.

AI Wingschool 03 · Phase 1 · The Challenge

Phase 1 · Free for everyone

A free AI aptitude challenge, hosted at your school.

One hour. Our proctors, our materials, our marking. A paper-or-tablet challenge testing logical reasoning, pattern recognition, and AI-readiness, calibrated by grade, Classes 5–10.

What it measures

Logic & patterns
Reasoning

Structured thinking under time: sequences, deduction, and the pattern-spotting that predicts how fast a student learns new tools.

Problem framing
Curiosity

How a student frames an unfamiliar problem, the single best early signal of builder potential.

The unknown
Builder instinct

Given something they've never seen, do they freeze or start taking it apart? We test for the second kind.

Every participant gets

  • A WingsQuest 2026 participation certificate, no child goes home empty-handed.
  • Their individual score band, shared privately.
  • An invitation to the results assembly at your school.
Your school gets: the report

Within 10 days of the Challenge: a school-level analytics report, participation, grade-wise performance distribution, strongest cohorts, and your school's standout students. The kind of data that goes straight into your annual report and your next parent-teacher meeting.
