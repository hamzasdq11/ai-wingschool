Brand guidelines.

The working identity of AI Wingschool, extracted from the product itself, the site, the wordmark, the type, the copy, and the imagery as they ship today. Use this manual to reproduce the brand faithfully across web, print, decks, and campaigns.

01 · Foundation

A school that gives teenagers wings.

AI Wingschool teaches teenagers in India, Classes 6 to 10, to think and build with AI. Its flagship, WingsQuest, pairs a free one-hour aptitude challenge with a live, project-based AI Builder Program mentored by IIT and IIM graduates, ending with every student presenting a real project on stage at Flagship Expo Day.

One idea runs through everything the brand does: flight. It is in the name, in the Latin tagline ad meliora, “toward better things”, in the painted figure leaping into the clouds, and in the wings mural behind the hero. The brand’s promise is altitude earned by building: aspiration with evidence, never hype.

Brand pillars, observable in every surface

Pillar 1

Building beats memorizing

The product of the program is a real, working AI project, not a syllabus. Copy, imagery, and even the FAQ answer (“technical confidence grows by making things, not by intimidation”) repeat this.

Pillar 2

Merit, transparently

Admission is “decided purely by score”; scholarships are stated as exact percentages for exact percentiles. The brand never softens or obscures the bar.

Pillar 3

Honesty over hype

Costs are named before they are due, “What if I don’t clear the cutoff?” gets a straight answer, and no superlative appears without a fact attached (IIT/IIM, 28 August, one hour).

Pillar 4

Built in India

“Designed and taught by IIT & IIM grads. Built for India.” The brand is proudly Indian, institutions, cities, boards, and an All-India percentile are first-class content.

Architecture & naming

The family

AI Wingschool is the organization (alternate short form: Wingschool). Under it: WingsQuest 2026 (the annual challenge), the AI Builder Program (the four-week live course), and Flagship Expo Day (the closing stage event).

Naming rules

Campaign properties compound with Wings- (WingsQuest, camel-cased, year appended: “WingsQuest 2026”). Programs stay plainly descriptive (AI Builder Program, Flagship Expo Day). The wordmark itself is always lowercase “ai wingschool”; in running text write “AI Wingschool”.

Audience

Students · Classes 6–10

Spoken to directly

“You’ve been using AI for years. Now find out if you’re built to build with it.” Energizing, second person, respectful of their intelligence, never childish.

Parents

Reassured with specifics

“It will touch every job your child applies for.” Fees, dates, mentor credentials, and time commitments are always concrete, the brand earns trust with numbers, not adjectives.

02 · Identity

A serif wordmark with a whispered motto.

The logo is a two-part lockup: “ai wingschool” in a classical old-style serif, all lowercase, and the motto “ad meliora” set in Editors Note Extralight Italic, tucked under the right edge. It ships as vector outlines (src/components/Logo.tsx, viewBox 141 × 40), never retype it, never substitute a font.

The serif does deliberate work: against an AI-education landscape of techy sans-serif brands, it reads like a school, considered, literate, permanent. Warmth comes from the cream it sits on, not from the mark itself.

Primary variants

Ink on light, default. Wordmark #0a0a0a · tagline #858585

Light on dark, footers, midnight panels. White 98% · tagline white 62%

Royal on white, email headers and one-color blue contexts (public/email/logo-blue.png)

Gray, quiet contexts such as email footers (public/email/logo-gray.png)

The “ai” monogram

Favicon: ink on cream tile · App icon: cream on ink tile

When to use it

The monogram is the first two glyphs of the wordmark on a rounded tile (corner radius ≈ 22% of the tile). It exists for spaces too small for the lockup: favicons, app icons, avatars, and the 28 px identity tile in this document’s header. The favicon uses the cream tile with ink glyphs; the home-screen icon inverts to an ink tile with cream glyphs. It never replaces the wordmark where the full name fits.

Clearspace & minimum size

Clearspace ≈ ¼ of wordmark height on all sides (recommended, matches its breathing room in the site nav)

Sizing rules

Default render height on the site is 44 px (nav and footer). Keep the lockup at 32 px or taller on screen, below that, “ad meliora” loses legibility; switch to the monogram tile instead. Scale only proportionally, from the SVG. The tagline is part of the mark: don’t detach, recolor, or reposition it independently.

Misuse, never do these

✕ Don’t recolor. Only ink, white, royal blue, and gray are approved fills.

✕ Don’t stretch or squash. Scale proportionally, always from the SVG.

ai wingschool

✕ Don’t retype it. The wordmark is drawn as outlines, Hanken Grotesk is not the logo.

✕ Don’t use ink on dark. On midnight or photos, use the light variant.

✕ Don’t add effects. No shadows, outlines, gradients, or bevels on the mark.

✕ Don’t sit it on busy photography. Add a scrim or move it to a quiet area first.

Cream, ink, and one blue.

Rules of use

Blue is an accent, not a ground

Roughly 90% of any page is cream, white, and ink. Royal blue appears as punctuation, a button, a chip, one word in a headline, and floods a surface only in the two gradient flagship panels.

Warm light, cool dark

Light surfaces are warm (cream family); dark surfaces are cool (midnight, navy overlays rgba(8,12,40,…)). Never mix a cool gray onto cream or a warm brown onto midnight.

Even the stars are blue

Ratings, live dots, list markers, details that other brands make gold or green stay royal blue here. One accent, everywhere, is what makes the system recognizable.

04 · Typography

One family, two voices.

Hanken Grotesk does everything, display, body, UI, in a single Regular (400) cut. Scale, tight tracking, and generous line-height create the hierarchy, not weight changes. Against it, Editors Note Extralight Italic appears for a word or two at a time: the emotional pivot of a headline, and the “ad meliora” motto itself.

The faces

Hanken Grotesk

Aa Bb Cc Dd Ee Ff Gg 0123456789

Geometric-humanist sans. Ships as Regular 400 only (HankenGrotesk-Regular.ttf). Display sizes always weight 400 with tracking −0.04 to −0.045em; small UI text may carry 500–600 for emphasis. Body sits at 1rem / 1.7.

Editors Note

Aa Bb Cc Dd Ee Ff Gg

Extralight Italic 200 (EditorsNote-ExtralightItalic.otf). An accent, never a workhorse: one italic phrase per heading at most, sized ~1.08–1.12× the surrounding text, colored #1F1F1F on light or white on dark. Never used for body copy, labels, or UI.

The italic accent, how it works

It marks the pivot

The script lands on the single word carrying the sentence’s emotion, and often takes the period with it:

Building beats memorizing.

One per heading

Never more than one italic phrase in a heading, never in body copy. In the hero it can grow to 1.12em:

Early starts win.

It survives on dark

On midnight and gradient panels the script turns white (95–100%), same rules:

WingsQuest 2026.

Practice notes

Weight discipline

Only Regular 400 is licensed and loaded. Displayed 500/600 in small UI (buttons, stat values) renders via browser synthesis, acceptable at ≤1rem, never at display sizes. If true medium/semibold cuts are added later, load them explicitly.

Tracking pairs with size

The bigger the type, the tighter it tracks: −0.045em at display, −0.03em at card level, neutral at body, then +0.16 to +0.24em expanded on uppercase micro-labels. Never track lowercase body text.

Numbers as graphics

Oversized numerals are a house motif: outlined “ghost” numerals (3px royal stroke at 40%), watermark digits at rgba(19,53,184,.07), and outlined years on gradient panels (white stroke 9%). Use them as structure, not decoration; they number real sequences.

05 · Voice & tone

Say the number. Skip the hype.

Wingschool writes in short, declarative sentences that treat both teenagers and parents as intelligent adults. Claims arrive with evidence attached, a date, a percentage, an institution, and the honest question gets answered before it’s asked. Every example below is live site copy.

Five principles, from the copy itself

Principle 1

Declarative rhythm

“One challenge. Four phases. One stage.”

Full stops do the work of exclamation marks, there isn’t a single “!” on the site. Short sentences land; long ones earn their length.

Principle 2

Specific beats superlative

“Applications close 15 August · Challenge Day 28 August.”

Dates, hours, percentages, and named institutions replace adjectives. “World-class” never appears; “IIT Madras” does.

Principle 3

Direct address, dual audience

“Now find out if you’re built to build with it.”

Students get an energizing “you”; parents get “your child” with reassurance built from facts. The same page speaks to both without switching register.

Principle 4

Honesty is the persuasion

“What if I don’t clear the cutoff?”

The brand raises the uncomfortable question itself and answers it straight, fees “shared directly with parents before anything is due”, no fine print.

Principle 5

Copy and type are one system

“Technical confidence grows by making things, not by intimidation.”

The italic script marks the emotional pivot of a headline; the em dash and the mid-dot (·) are the house punctuation for asides and paired facts. Write the sentence so the pivot word is obvious, if you can’t find the word to italicize, rewrite the sentence.

Do / don’t

✓ Write like this

“A free one-hour online test, then a live AI Builder Program mentored by IIT & IIM graduates.”

“Hardware included: Arduino, sensors, robotics, shipped to your door.”

“Builder Program seats are awarded on merit alone.”

✕ Never like this

“An amazing, world-class AI bootcamp that will transform your child’s future!!”

“Hurry, limited spots, don’t miss out on this incredible opportunity!”

Anything that hides the fee, buries the cutoff, or promises outcomes it can’t number.

06 · UI components

Pills, cards, and midnight panels.

Every interactive element is a fully-rounded pill; every container is a generously-rounded card. The demos below are live replicas of the shipped CSS, hover them.

Forms

Form conventions

Labels are 0.75rem at 62% ink above the field. Placeholders #999999. Validation is inline, per field, in #B3261E. Forms carry a honeypot for bots, an OTP email-verification step, and a WhatsApp/email fallback when submission fails, resilience is part of the design language.

House details

Corner radius scale

Base token 1.25rem. Inputs 0.9rem · cards 1.5–1.75rem · flagship panels 2.5rem · anything interactive and small: 999px (full pill). Sharp corners don’t exist in this brand.

Hairline dividers

Sections separate with a 1px line that fades at both ends: transparent → 9% ink 24–76% → transparent. Never a solid full-width rule.

Live dots

A 6–8px royal dot with a CSS ping halo marks “happening now” items (Flagship Expo Day, live chips). Use at most one per view.

Shadows are tinted

Elevation follows content: ink-tinted for neutral cards (2–4% opacity), royal-tinted under blue elements (rgba(19,53,184,.2–.32)). Never plain black at high opacity.

07 · Imagery & illustration

Real builders, painted skies.

Two registers, one motif. Documentary photography shows real students mid-build and mid-presentation, candid, naturally lit, never stock-posed. Painterly illustration carries the emotional register: skies, clouds, and wings. Both serve the flight idea; neither is decorated with tech clichés (no circuit-brains, no glowing robots).

The three image families

Documentary, proofExpo floors, laptops open, projects mid-demo. This is the OG/share image and the hero video’s register: evidence that students really build and really present.

Wings, the motif made literalThe hero image: a student framed by handmade wings. Joyful, colorful, human, the brand idea photographed rather than illustrated.

Painterly flight, aspirationThe footer artwork: a figure leaping into a blue sky. Fine-art texture, not vector flatness. Used under navy scrims (50–94%) as an atmospheric ground.

Treatment in layouts

The hero treatment

Photos sit behind, at 22–30% opacity, under a gradient mask.

Live demo: image at 26% opacity, masked to transparent at the base, type at full contrast on cream

Treatment rules

Photography is never a full-bleed background with text punched over it. Either it lives inside a card (rounded 1.25–2rem, often inside a white frame card tilted −3°), or it fades behind cream at 22–30% opacity with a linear mask. On dark, imagery goes under a navy scrim (rgba(8,12,40,.5–.94)) so white type stays legible. Videos autoplay muted with a poster fallback, and reduced-motion users get the still.

Proof marks

Institutional logos

IIT Madras, IIT Ropar, IIM Bangalore, and IIM Ranchi logos (in public/LOGOS/) appear as credentials, always in their own original form, on light grounds, sized modestly in rows. Never recolored, never implying the institutions endorse the program beyond mentor alumni status.

Sourcing new imagery

Shoot or select: real students, real hardware (Arduino, sensors, robots), natural light, mid-action. Avoid: posed stock, blue-toned “AI” abstracts, code rain, and anything where the student is a passive screen-watcher. If it doesn’t show building or flight, it isn’t a Wingschool image.

08 · Layout & motion

A calm shell, a rising reveal.

Pages breathe. Sections are separated by air and hairlines, copy never exceeds a comfortable measure, and everything enters with the same gesture: a gentle rise and fade.

Metrics

ElementSpecNotes
Section shellmax-width 84rem · padding 7rem 1.5remThe core rhythm, roughly one viewport of air per section
Copy measuremax-width 42remHeadings and body cap here (~65ch) even in wide sections
Nav / hero containermax-width 80remSticky nav: white 85% + blur, hairline bottom border
Anchor offsetscroll-margin-top 5.5remSections clear the sticky nav when linked
Card grid gap1.1–1.25remCards in 2–4 column grids, collapsing to one on mobile

Motion vocabulary

fade-rise

translateY(24px)→0 + fade · 0.8s ease-out, staggered in 0.2s steps for hero elements. The single entry gesture: nothing slides sideways, nothing bounces.

Scroll reveal

Sections reveal once on first view: 26px rise · 0.9s · cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1), delays of 80–120ms between siblings, triggered 60px before entering view.

Hover

Buttons and swatch-like elements lift −1px with a deepened shadow; arrows nudge +4px right. Cards themselves stay still.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion is honored everywhere: reveals render instantly, the hero video swaps to its poster frame. Non-negotiable.

Canonical applications

Wordmark PNGs in public/email/: blue on white for headers, gray for footers, white for dark headers. Sender voice matches the site; OTP codes go in the subject line so they’re readable from the inbox list.

Print & decks

The brochure and school proposal use the same tokens on print cream #FBFAF6, Hanken Grotesk for text, Editors Note for accents, royal blue as the single accent, midnight for cover/gravity pages.

WhatsApp & support

WhatsApp is a first-class brand channel (wa.me/917355080850), pill-shaped entry points with the glyph, promising “2-min reply”. Keep responses in brand voice: short, specific, no exclamation marks.

Campaign links

Every distributed URL carries ?utm_source=… or ?ref=…; attribution is part of publishing, not an afterthought. Links without it report as “direct / unknown”.

Structured data

The org is named consistently everywhere: AI Wingschool (alternate “Wingschool”), EducationalOrganization schema, logo = the blue email wordmark. Keep names and dates in schema synced with page copy.
